2. Subject-wise syllabus – all units

Complete topic list for all 6 subjects, organized unit by unit. Select a subject to expand its units.
6 Subjects · 30 Units
Environmental Studies (EVS)
25UCT-201 · 5 Units
  1. Unit 1 – Introduction & Ecosystem: Environmental studies: definition, concepts, scope and importance; environmental risk principles; environmental significance of GM organisms/biotechnology; integrated approach and market mechanisms; ecosystem structure and functions; indicator and keystone species; biodiversity and ecosystem services.
  2. Unit 2 – Environmental Pollution: Introduction to environmental pollution; air and noise pollution; water and soil pollution; sources, effects and control; nuclear hazards and thermal pollution; solid-waste management; 3R concept; biomagnification and eutrophication.
  3. Unit 3 – Climate Change: Climate change and global warming; acid rain and natural disasters; global climate-change projections; impacts, vulnerability and adaptation; climate-change mitigation and national initiatives; AI algorithms for climate prediction.
  4. Unit 4 – Ethics, Conservation & Society: Environmental ethics and ecological values; role of Indian religions and cultures in conservation; environmental communication and public awareness; energy conservation and water conservation; environmental movements including Chipko, Silent Valley and Bishnoi; resettlement and rehabilitation.
  5. Unit 5 – Environmental Laws: Introduction to environmental laws and policies; Environment (Protection) Act, 1986; Noise Pollution Rules and Biomedical Waste Management Rules; role of magistracy, higher courts and regulatory bodies; international agreements including Montreal and Kyoto protocols; Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR).
Data Structures (DSA)
25CSH-209 · 5 Units
  1. Unit 1 – Data Structures, Arrays & Linked Lists: Overview of data structures; primitive and non-primitive data types; algorithms and efficiency; best, worst and average-case complexity; asymptotic notations; time-space trade-off; ADT; arrays and multidimensional arrays; row-major and column-major representation; sparse matrices; singly, doubly and circular linked lists with insertion, deletion and traversal.
  2. Unit 2 – Stack & Queue: Stack and push/pop operations; array and linked-list implementation; applications of stack; prefix and postfix expressions and postfix evaluation; recursion and tail recursion; iteration vs recursion; Fibonacci and Tower of Hanoi; queues and their operations; deque, circular queue and priority queue.
  3. Unit 3 – Searching, Hashing & Sorting: Searching concepts and efficiency; linear and binary searching; hashing concepts and techniques; collision resolution; insertion sort, selection sort, quick sort, heap sort, merge sort, radix sort and shell sort.
  4. Unit 4 – Trees, Balanced Trees & Heaps: Tree definitions and properties; binary-tree representation using arrays and linked lists; BST; strict, complete, full, extended and skewed binary trees; inorder, preorder and postorder traversal; BST insertion, deletion, searching and updation; Huffman coding; AVL trees and rotations; B-Trees; binary heaps.
  5. Unit 5 – Graphs & Graph Algorithms: Graph concepts, terminology and types; adjacency matrix and adjacency list; DFS and BFS; connected components; spanning trees and minimum spanning trees; Prim’s and Kruskal’s algorithms; single-source and all-pairs shortest-path algorithm concepts.
Discrete Mathematics
25MTT-202 · 5 Units
  1. Unit 1 – Mathematical Logic & Proof: Propositions and compound propositions; logical operators and truth tables; laws of logic and logical equivalence; tautology, contradiction and contingency; implication and biconditional; universal and existential quantifiers; rules of inference; proof by contradiction; mathematical induction.
  2. Unit 2 – Sets & Functions: Introduction to sets and types of sets; subsets and power sets; Venn diagrams and set operations; Cartesian product; functions and their types; one-to-one, onto and bijective functions; composition, inverse and recursive functions.
  3. Unit 3 – Relations & Boolean Algebra: Binary relations and their properties; reflexive, symmetric, antisymmetric and transitive relations; equivalence relations and equivalence classes; partitions of sets; partial ordering relations; Hasse diagrams; lattices and their properties; Boolean algebra; applications in digital logic.
  4. Unit 4 – Counting & Recurrence: Basic counting principles; addition and multiplication rules; permutations and combinations; binomial theorem and Pascal’s identity; pigeonhole principle; inclusion-exclusion principle; recurrence relations and linear recurrence; generating functions; applications in algorithm analysis.
  5. Unit 5 – Graph Theory: Introduction to graphs and types of graphs; degree of a vertex, paths and circuits; Euler graphs; Hamiltonian graphs; trees and their properties; spanning trees and minimum spanning trees; graph coloring; applications of graph theory.
Python Programming
25CSH-214 · 5 Units
  1. Unit 1 – Computer Programs & Python Basics: Need for programming; human and computer programming languages; computer hardware/software interaction; interpreter and compiler; reserved words; history and installation of Python; Python prompt and traceback; writing a Python program; input/output; sequential, conditional and repeated execution; reuse through functions; types of errors.
  2. Unit 2 – Values, Variables & Debugging: Values and data types; variables and naming rules; keywords and statements; operators and expressions; order of operations; conditional execution; Boolean expressions; chained and nested conditionals; try-except and debugging.
  3. Unit 3 – Built-in & User-defined Functions: Built-in functions; type conversion; math functions; user-defined functions; parameters and arguments; creating functions for business applications; financial statement analysis; goodwill and related business calculations.
  4. Unit 4 – Loops, Files & Collections: While and for loops; updating variables; file operations – open, read, search and write text files; lists, traversal, operations and slicing; dictionaries and their operations/file use; tuples, immutability and comparison.
  5. Unit 5 – GUI with Tkinter: Introduction to GUI applications and Tkinter; adding widgets – Label, Entry and Button; event handling and button clicks; mouse events; layout management using pack, grid and place.
Database Management System (DBMS)
25CSH-211 · 5 Units
  1. Unit 1 – DBMS & Relational Model: Introduction to DBMS and database systems; three-schema architecture and data independence; relational data model and concepts; super key, candidate key, primary key, foreign key and other keys; entity and referential integrity constraints; relational algebra – selection, projection, union, intersection, difference, Cartesian product, join, division, rename and extended operations; tuple relational calculus (TRC); domain relational calculus (DRC); equivalence of relational queries.
  2. Unit 2 – ER Model & Normalization: Entity-Relationship modeling; weak entities, specialization, generalization and aggregation; mapping ER model to relational model; functional dependencies and their types; attribute closure and candidate keys; 1NF, 2NF, 3NF, BCNF, 4NF and 5NF; lossless join and dependency preservation.
  3. Unit 3 – SQL & Database Programming: DDL commands – CREATE, ALTER, DROP and related commands; DML – INSERT, UPDATE and DELETE; SELECT query formulation; GROUP BY, HAVING and aggregate functions; inner and outer joins; nested and correlated subqueries; views and constraints; triggers and stored procedures; indexing commands and transaction control.
  4. Unit 4 – Transactions & Concurrency: Transaction concepts and ACID properties; schedules and types of schedules; conflict and view serializability; precedence graphs and correctness; concurrency control and two-phase locking (2PL); timestamp ordering; deadlocks; logging, checkpointing and crash recovery.
  5. Unit 5 – Storage, Indexing & Modern Databases: Physical storage organization and file structures; B-Tree and B+ Tree indexing; multilevel indexing and performance; query-processing architecture and cost-based optimization; NoSQL databases; CAP theorem; database security and related concepts.
Computer Organization & Architecture (COA)
25CST-208 · 5 Units
  1. Unit 1 – Introduction to Digital Fundamentals: Number systems – decimal, binary, octal and hexadecimal; base conversions; binary arithmetic (addition and subtraction); signed and unsigned numbers; logic gates; K-Map for 2, 3 and 4 variables; adders and subtractors; multiplexers, encoders and decoders; sequential circuits – flip-flops, registers and counters.
  2. Unit 2 – Register Organization: Von Neumann architecture; register transfer and micro-operations; register transfer language (RTL); arithmetic, logic and shift micro-operations; bus and memory transfers; instruction cycle and computer registers; common bus system and computer instructions; addressing modes; basic computer design; general register organization; stack organization; instruction formats; data transfer and manipulation; program control; RISC and CISC.
  3. Unit 3 – Computer Arithmetic: Hardware implementation of addition and subtraction; multiplication algorithms; Booth’s multiplier; array multiplier; division algorithms; floating-point arithmetic and representation; floating-point addition, subtraction, multiplication and division.
  4. Unit 4 – Memory & I/O Organization: Memory hierarchy; RAM and ROM chips; memory address map and memory connection; associative memory and cache memory; virtual memory, paging and segmentation; auxiliary memory; magnetic disks and magnetic tapes; input-output interface; asynchronous data transfer; modes of transfer; priority interrupt; DMA and IOP.
  5. Unit 5 – Hardwired/Microprogrammed Control & Pipelining: Hardwired control; microprogrammed control; microinstructions and microprogram sequencing; pipelining; parallel processing and pipeline structure; speedup and efficiency; throughput and bottlenecks; arithmetic pipeline and instruction pipeline concepts.
Syllabus source: Subject names, course codes and unit topics are organized from the uploaded Chandigarh University “All Subjects Semester 3rd” lecture-plan PDF. Topic wording is lightly cleaned for readability; refer to the official course document if any wording differs.
